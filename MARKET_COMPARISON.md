## What the table says

- **Closest references.** Slack, Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, Zulip and Matrix share Chatney's model: workspaces, channels, threads, roles and an admin console. Mattermost, Rocket.Chat, Zulip and Matrix are also self-hosted, so self-hosting sets Chatney apart from the consumer messengers only.
- **Where Chatney is ahead of the team-chat tools.** Media. Chatney has voice messages, video messages and the "send as file or compressed" choice. None of the five team-chat tools has all three for free: Rocket.Chat lacks the compression choice, Slack keeps clips on paid plans, and Mattermost and Zulip have none of them. Chatney's permissions are also free where Slack, Mattermost and Rocket.Chat charge for custom roles or per-channel overrides.
- **What every team-chat tool has and Chatney lacks (27 rows).** Details panel, membership, sidebar control, profile card, text formatting, mentions, drafts, up arrow to edit, slash commands, copy text and link, forward, see who reacted, quick switcher, browse and join channels, unread counts, "new messages" divider, mark as read / unread, notifications, notification level per chat, presence, typing indicator, delivery state, settings page, keyboard shortcuts help, installable app, bots and integrations, data export.
- **What all nine have and Chatney lacks (12 rows).** The hardest table stakes: membership, profile card, mentions, see who reacted, unread counts, notifications, notification level per chat, presence, typing indicator, delivery state, settings page, data export.
- **Rare everywhere.** "Also send to channel" (Slack and Rocket.Chat only), selecting several messages (Telegram and WhatsApp only), a do-not-disturb schedule (Slack only) and the compression choice. These can sit at the bottom of the backlog.
- **Snapchat is the outlier.** It is built around disappearing one-to-one chats, so it skips most of the search, organisation and moderation rows.

## Why a cell is ⚠️

**Chatney**
- Rename, archive or delete: only in the admin area, not from the chat.
- Moderation: an admin can mute or ban a user and a role can delete anyone's messages. No slow mode, timeouts, reports or audit log.

**Telegram**
- Channel groups: personal chat folders, and topics inside one group.
- Roles: admin rights and member permissions are set per group. There are no reusable roles.
- Reply threads: comments under channel posts, and topics in groups.
- Repliers' avatars: only on the comments button under channel posts.
- Custom status: an emoji status, for Premium subscribers only.

**WhatsApp**
- Workspaces and channel groups: a Community puts several group chats under one roof.
- Roles: admin or member only.
- Reply threads and repliers' avatars: threaded replies with a reply count were in beta from 2025, with no confirmed general release.
- Scheduled and silent send: scheduling was in beta on Android and iOS in 2026, with no confirmed general release. No silent send.
- Copy link to message: text can be copied, but a message has no link.
- Moderation: admins can delete messages, restrict who posts, and receive member reports. No slow mode, timeouts or audit log.
- Bots: only through the WhatsApp Business Platform.

**Discord**
- Voice messages: mobile app only.
- Admin console: each server has settings for roles, members and channels. There is no management of user accounts.
- Do not disturb: a status that can be set for a period, not a repeating schedule.
- Scheduled and silent send: `@silent` sends without a notification. No scheduling.
- Save and remind: bookmarks and reminders exist, for Nitro subscribers only.
- Jump to a date: through the `before:`, `during:` and `after:` search filters, not a calendar.
- Media browser: through search filters, not a separate shared-media view.
- Offline outbox: a message sent offline fails and offers retry.

**Snapchat**
- Channels: Topic Chats only. Everything else is a group chat.
- Any file: photos and videos, not arbitrary files.
- Edit: within five minutes of sending. It launched for Snapchat+ subscribers.
- Uploads: several photos from the gallery on mobile. The web version is limited.
- Polls: poll stickers, and polls inside Topic Chats.
- Copy link to message: text can be copied, but a message has no link.
- Forward: media only.
- Save and remind: "Save in Chat" keeps a message. No reminders on a message.
- Media browser: shows only what was saved in the chat.
- Browse and join: Topic Chats only.
- Details panel: name and members, no topic or description.
- Rename, archive or delete: a group can be renamed and left, not deleted.
- Sidebar control: pin up to three conversations (iOS only, per Snapchat's help page) and clear a chat from the feed.
- Calls: voice and video calls, no screen sharing.

**Slack**
- Channel groups: each person arranges their own sidebar sections, on paid plans.
- Roles: fixed roles (owner, admin, member, guest). Custom roles are Enterprise only.
- Group chats: adding a person starts a new conversation.
- Moderation: audit logs and message flagging are Enterprise only. No slow mode or timeouts.
- Disappearing messages: retention periods per channel, on paid plans.
- Reply with a quote, and jump to the original: replies go into a thread. There is no quoted reply in the channel.
- Scheduled and silent send: scheduling only.
- Polls: through apps or workflows.
- Voice messages, video messages, and playback speed with transcript: audio and video clips, on paid plans.
- Report a message: flagging content for admins is Enterprise only.
- Translate: part of the paid AI features.

**Mattermost**
- Channel groups: each person arranges their own sidebar categories.
- Roles: built-in roles with editable permissions. Per-team and per-channel overrides need a paid plan.
- Read-only channels: channel moderation needs a paid plan.
- Group chats: adding a person starts a new conversation. A group message can be converted to a private channel.
- Moderation, and report a message: content flagging and audit logging are Enterprise features. No slow mode or timeouts.
- Disappearing messages: burn-on-read and retention policies are Enterprise features.
- Reply with a quote, and jump to the original: replies go into a thread. There is no quoted reply in the channel.
- Scheduled and silent send: scheduling on the Entry edition and paid plans. No silent send.
- Polls: through a plugin.
- Audio player: audio plays in the file preview, without a waveform.
- Edit history: you can see and restore earlier versions of your own message. Other people cannot see them.
- Jump to a date: through the `before:`, `on:` and `after:` search filters.
- Do not disturb: a status with an end time, not a repeating schedule.
- Read receipts: acknowledgement requests on priority messages, on paid plans.
- Calls and drop-in rooms: voice calls with screen sharing through the built-in Calls plugin. Limits depend on the edition.
- Social sign-in or SSO: paid plans or the Entry edition.

**Rocket.Chat**
- Workspaces and channel groups: a Team groups channels and members inside one workspace.
- Roles: permissions of the built-in roles can be edited. Custom roles need a paid plan.
- Moderation: message reports with a moderation console, and muting a user in a room. No slow mode or timeouts.
- Several files: drag-and-drop and paste work, but one file per message.
- Polls: through a marketplace app.
- Location and contact sharing: location only.
- Save and remind: starring only.
- Edit history: kept only if the admin turns it on.
- Search: one room at a time.
- Activity inbox: mentions and threads are listed per room.
- Read receipts: paid plans.
- Calls: video through a conferencing provider such as Jitsi, voice through Rocket.Chat Voice.

**Zulip**
- Workspaces: one server can host several organisations, but each has separate accounts.
- Moderation: message reports go to a moderation channel (since version 12). No slow mode or timeouts.
- Scheduled and silent send: scheduling only.
- Reply threads: every message belongs to a topic inside a channel, which does the job of a thread.
- Media browser: through search filters such as `has:attachment`.
- Calls: a call button opens Jitsi, Zoom or BigBlueButton.
- Security: two-factor sign-in is a beta server setting.

**Matrix (Element)**
- Profile: avatar and display name, no bio.
- Rename, archive or delete: a room can be renamed and left, but its members cannot delete it.
- Moderation: kick, ban, remove messages, and report to the server admin. No slow mode or audit log.
- Emoji extras: stickers and recent emoji. No GIF picker or custom emoji.
- Location and contact sharing: location only.
- Send as file or compressed: offered in the mobile apps only.
- Follow a thread: threads show unread state, but there is no follow switch.
- Search: encrypted rooms can be searched only in the desktop app.
- Jump to a date: needs server support and started as an experimental option.
- Activity inbox: a notifications panel, and a thread list per room.
- Custom status: experimental.
- Drop-in rooms: video rooms, in beta.
- Security: session list and device verification. Two-factor sign-in depends on the server's sign-in provider.
- Admin console: an admin API and separate admin tools, not part of Element.

## Not confirmed

These cells are my best judgement and were not confirmed on the web:

- **Snapchat:** `:name` autocomplete, arbitrary files, whether editing is still limited to Snapchat+, "new messages" divider, drafts, text formatting, forward, jump to the original from a reply, voice-note speed and transcript, translate, message search, screen sharing, shortcuts help, social sign-in, bots.
- **WhatsApp:** up arrow to edit on desktop.
- **Discord:** whether do not disturb can repeat on a schedule.
- **Slack:** which plans include sidebar sections and clips.
- **Mattermost:** which plan read-only channels need, voice messages, the audio player, the do-not-disturb schedule, and the Calls limits per edition.
- **Rocket.Chat:** jump to a date, adding people to an existing group message, audio playback speed, and whether retention policies are free.
- **Zulip:** pinned messages, jump to a date, voice messages, the inline audio player.
- **Matrix (Element):** whether jump to a date is on by default, custom status, bookmarks, voice-message speed, the compression choice, video rooms.
